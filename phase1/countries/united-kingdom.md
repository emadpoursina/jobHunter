# Country: United Kingdom (GB)

- **Priority rank:** 3
- **Target role:** Full-stack / backend JavaScript developer (Senior Backend Engineer band)
- **Migration route via job offer:** Skilled Worker visa — employer must hold a valid Home Office sponsor licence; software developers / programmers (SOC 2136) on the eligible occupation list
- **Language requirement:** English (native country) — meets the English-language requirement by default
- **Sponsorship common?:** Yes — large Node/React employer base with thousands of licensed sponsors (big tech, banks, fintech, startups); route requires an employer-issued Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS)
- **Known blockers:** Employer MUST be a licensed sponsor (check the Home Office register); salary must meet the general threshold AND the "going rate" for the occupation; visa fees + Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) add significant cost; London market is competitive
- **Primary job boards / sources:**
  - LinkedIn, Indeed.co.uk, Reed, Totaljobs, WorkInStartups, Otta
  - Relocate.me, VisaSponsor.jobs, UK Government register of licensed sponsors
- **Notes (updated Aug 2026):**
  - **General salary threshold: £38,700/yr** — but you must be paid the **higher of** the general threshold (£38,700) **or the occupation's "going rate."** For software development professionals the going rate typically EXCEEDS the £38,700 floor, so check the current Going Rates table for SOC 2136 (Programmers and Software Development Professionals) at application time. Total remuneration counted includes base salary + qualifying allowances (excluding overtime/bonuses above 20%).
  - **Threshold context:** The £38,700 general figure covers most skilled roles (increased threshold policy from 2024). Note: a lower "new entrant" threshold (£30,960 — 70% of going rate) applies to those under 26 or on a graduate route — not typically relevant here. **Emad's opening ask £80–95K (master-profile §9) clears both the £38,700 threshold and the software going rate comfortably.**
  - **Getting the job:** Employer must be on the **Home Office Register of Licensed Sponsors** to issue a CoS. Filter job boards toward employers that advertise sponsorship; smaller employers often don't hold a licence. No Labour Market Test for skilled roles at/below threshold.
  - **Costs (applicant):** Skilled Worker application fee (~£719–£1,420 depending on route) + **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) ~£1,035/yr** (18-month+ in one payment) + biometric fee. Budget these into any offer negotiation.
  - **PR route:** ILR (Indefinite Leave to Remain) after **5 years** on Skilled Worker (salary must meet ILR-specific threshold, currently £38,700/going rate); citizenship after ILR + time. Spouse/children can join as dependants.
  - **Iranian nationals:** The Skilled Worker route has **no blanket restriction** for Iranian nationals — no blanket ban observed as of Aug 2026; re-verify current policy before applying.
  - **Going rate caveat:** The precise SOC 2136 going-rate figure could not be re-verified from a clean source this pass — confirm against the current gov.uk Going Rates table during job search. Emad's band sits comfortably above the general threshold regardless.
