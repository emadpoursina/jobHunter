# Country: Ireland (IE)

- **Priority rank:** 2
- **Target role:** Full-stack / backend JavaScript developer (Senior Backend Engineer band)
- **Migration route via job offer:** Critical Skills Employment Permit (CSEP) — software developers / ICT professionals are on the Critical Skills Occupations List
- **Language requirement:** English (native country)
- **Sponsorship common?:** Yes — European HQ for Google, Meta, Apple, Amazon, Microsoft, Stripe (large international + startup scene); CSEP is employer-sponsored and does NOT require a Labour Market Needs Test
- **Known blockers:** High Dublin living costs; salary must meet CSEP remuneration threshold; cost-of-living/housing pressure in Dublin metro
- **Primary job boards / sources:**
  - LinkedIn, Indeed.ie, IrishJobs.ie, Glassdoor.ie
- **Notes (updated Aug 2026):**
  - **CSEP remuneration thresholds (official DETE, 2026):** €40,904/yr for occupations on the Critical Skills Occupations List (restricted list) with a relevant degree or 3+ yrs experience; €36,848 for recent graduates (qualification within 12 months); €68,911 for ALL other occupations not on the Ineligible List. Basic salary is the core — overtime/bonuses largely not counted toward the threshold. **Software developers sit on the CSOL, so the €40,904 threshold applies — Emad's senior-band ask is comfortably above it.**
  - **Consultation/review active:** DETE ran a review of the occupations lists in 2025 (report: "Employment Permits Occupations Lists Review 2025"). Confirm the CSOL still lists your exact title at application time — lists are reviewed and updated.
  - **CSEP benefits:** 2-year permit, no Labour Market Needs Test required (faster, employer-friendly), immediate family can accompany (no separate work-permit for spouse). After **2 years** on CSEP you can apply for **Stamp 4** (no permit needed to change employer / stay) — then PR (Stamp 5/4) after further residence. Citizenship after 5 years.
  - **Alternative long-term route:** General Employment Permit (GEP) for roles on the Ineligible-adjacent list at €34,000 threshold — only relevant if a role is NOT on the CSOL; CSEP is the preferred path for tech.
  - **Salary:** Senior backend engineer Ireland avg ≈ €60K/yr (talent.com, 2026); master-profile opening ask €65–75K, floor €55K — all above the €40,904 CSOL tech threshold. Dublin metro commands the higher end.
  - **Fee:** CSEP processing fee €1,000 (90% refunded if unsuccessful).
