# Country: Spain (ES)

- **Priority rank:** Contingency only — remote-first Digital Nomad Visa strategy; not part of the active sponsored-employer ranking
- **Target role:** Full-stack / backend JavaScript developer (Senior Backend Engineer band) OR remote worker on Digital Nomad visa
- **Migration route via job offer:**
  - **Digital Nomad Visa (DNV)** — *the standout option for Emad:* live in Spain while working remotely for a **non-Spanish** employer (or 80% non-Spanish freelance clients). No Spanish employer/sponsor needed. Regulated under Ley 14/2013 (Startups Law, amended 2023); new "Spain 2030" successor law passed 2025.
  - **Highly Qualified Professional (HQP / "cuenta ajena" / International Mobility unit)** work permit — for skilled workers hired by a Spanish employer; salary ≈ €40,077–€41,356/yr (2026), higher for directors.
  - **EU Blue Card (Spain)** — ≥ ~€40–45K gross/yr (≈1.5× avg salary), reduced threshold for STEM etc.
  - **Standard work permit (arraigo/cuenta ajena)** — generic employer-sponsored route, higher bar / less privileged.
- **Language requirement:** Spanish helpful but NOT required for DNV; English is workable in Madrid/Barcelona tech + remote. Spanish useful (A2–B1) for long-term local integration.
- **Sponsorship common?:** The **DNV needs NO Spanish sponsor** — this is why Spain differs from Emad's other targets (Canada/Ireland/UK/NL/PT all require employer sponsorship). For the HQP/standard employer routes sponsorship exists but is less common than Ireland/UK big-tech density.
- **Known blockers:** Spanish tech salaries are **the lowest of all options** (senior backend ≈ €37–45K/yr vs €65–80K+ elsewhere) — meaningful income drop; **Beckham Law / special 24% flat tax** only applies in the DNV path and only to income ≤ €600K for up to 5 yrs; Spanish bureaucracy is slow; no ILR-equivalent automatic PR advantage.
- **Primary job boards / sources:**
  - LinkedIn, Indeed.es, InfoJobs
  - DNV/remote: RemoteOK, WeWorkRemotely, job boards filtered to "digital nomad" / remote-first
- **Notes (updated Aug 2026):**
  - **DNV income requirement (2026):** 200% of SMI = **€2,849/month gross (~€34,188/yr)** for the primary applicant; +75% SMI for a spouse/partner; +25% SMI per dependent child. Emad's senior-band income qualifies easily.
  - **DNV key conditions:** (1) remote work/freelance client base for **non-Spanish companies**; (2) work *can* be performed remotely (employer letter confirming); (3) has NOT been a Spanish resident in the previous 5 years; (4) clean criminal record (home country + no EU crime); (5) private/comprehensive health insurance; (6) 3+ months' remote contract or client evidence. Can apply at Spanish consulate abroad or inside Spain (estancia/modelo).
  - **DNV tax (Beckham Law):** on approval can opt into the special regime — flat **24% tax** on up to €600K/yr for up to 5 years (vs progressive ~37–47% marginal). Big lever for a high earner.
  - **DNV duration/PR:** renewable residency path **1 + 3 + 2 years**; counts toward long-term/EU long-term residence after **5 years** of residence. Family can accompany.
  - **HQP threshold (2026):** salary ≈ €40,077–€41,356/yr (two tiers: professionals vs managers/directors). Below Emad's senior band in Spain — fine to meet, but comp is the issue, not the threshold.
  - **EU Blue Card (Spain):** ≥ ~€40–45K gross/yr (≈1.5× average national salary); reduced thresholds for shortage/STEM occupations; faster path to EU long-term residence (also ~5 yr residency, with Blue Card rules).
  - **Spain 2030 (Ley 14/2013 successor):** passed Oct 2025 — designed to keep/attract digital nomads, entrepreneurs and investors; extends/refines the 2023 regime. Confirm current DNV rules under the 2030 law when applying (income figure may be re-indexed to SMI annually).
  - **Iranian nationals:** no blanket visa restriction observed for the DNV/HQP/Blue Card paths as of Aug 2026 — re-verify at application (Spain grants third-country work/residence on standard EU basis; Iranians require a visa for Spain).
  - **Income note for Emad:** DNV lets Emad keep a **non-Spanish**, higher-paying job (e.g. a remote role from the UK/Netherlands/Ireland/Germany market) while living in lower-cost Spain — this is the key strategic selling point vs employer-sponsored-only countries.
